    🔗
    1. \(\displaystyle \frac{d}{dx}(f(x)\cdot g(x)) = \left(\frac{d}{dx}f(x)\right)\cdot \left(\frac{d}{dx}g(x)\right)\)

    2. \(\displaystyle \frac{d}{dx}(f(x)\cdot g(x)) = f(x)\cdot\left(\frac{d}{dx}g(x)\right) + g(x)\cdot\left(\frac{d}{dx}f(x)\right)\)

    3.

    Let \(f\) and \(g\) be two differentiable functions. Circle the correct Quotient Rule for derivatives.
    🔗
    1. \(\displaystyle \frac{d}{dx}\left(\frac{f(x)}{g(x)}\right) = \frac{\frac{d}{dx}(f(x))} {\frac{d}{dx}(g(x))}\)
      🔗
      🔗
    2. \(\displaystyle \frac{d}{dx}\left(\frac{f(x)}{g(x)}\right) = \frac{ g(x)\cdot \left(\frac{d}{dx}f(x)\right) - f(x)\cdot \left(\frac{d}{dx}g(x)\right) }{g(x)}\)
      🔗
      🔗
    3. \(\displaystyle \frac{d}{dx}\left(\frac{f(x)}{g(x)}\right) = \frac{ g(x)\cdot \left(\frac{d}{dx}f(x)\right) - f(x)\cdot \left(\frac{d}{dx}g(x)\right) }{(g(x))^2}\)
