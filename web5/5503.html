    Getting Warmed Up and in the Right Mindset.

    1.

    Fill in the blanks to complete the special cases of the chain rule.
    The Chain Rule: If \(f\) and \(g\) are differentiable functions, then
    \begin{equation*} \frac{d}{dx}(f(g(x))) = f'(g(x))\cdot g'(x). \end{equation*}
    🔗
    Special Cases of Chain Rule: If \(g\) is a differentiable function, \(n\) is any real number, and \(b\) is any positive real number, then
    • Generalized Power Rule: If \(\displaystyle{y =(g(x))^n}\text{,}\) then \(\D y\;'= \rule{2in}{0.05mm}.\)
      🔗
      🔗
    • Generalized Exponential (base \(b\)) Rule: If \(\displaystyle{y =b^{g(x)}}\text{,}\) then \(\D y\;'= \rule{2in}{0.05mm}.\)
      🔗
      🔗
    • Generalized Logarithm (base \(b\)) Rule: If \(\displaystyle{y =\log_b(g(x))}\text{,}\) then \(\D y\;'=\rule{2in}{0.05mm}.\)
      🔗
      🔗
